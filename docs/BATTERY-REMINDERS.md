# Low-battery charge reminders

Tracking: [shared epic](https://github.com/open-horizon-labs/embedded-services/issues/20), [PearlPod integration](https://github.com/open-horizon-labs/PearlPod/issues/4). PearlPod pins the shared embedded-services `device_battery` sampler/policy and `device_battery_ui` banner. Its adapter supplies ADC1 channel0 / GPIO1, a 2× divider and the existing atomic display-wake request. Sampling and UI work stay with the LVGL owner; the button/power task services wake requests without reentrant display locking. No duplicate ADC or warning implementation was added to this repository.

Hardware evidence: upstream demo defines BAT_ADC_PIN1 for the CS43131 board. To establish divider scaling rather than guess from another board, inspected the manufacturer's [FAKE_POD_NANO project](https://oshwhub.com/planevina/fakepodnano-lossless-audioplayer) attachment `PROJECT_N7_IDF_CS43131_1.3.2_0x10000_ota.bin`, SHA256 `91147a8f25ab17aa1ae00f21daec44fdf30e0d9c8b8386a5aa28ed8ede803868`. The ADC battery-estimation create function is at 0x4205353c; its resistor configuration fields are offsets16 and20. Caller 0x4200c233 loads literal0x461c4000 (float10000.0), stores it in both fields at 0x4200c23f and 0x4200c241, then calls create at 0x4200c24d. Equal upper/lower10k resistors establish 2× scaling. This confirms manufacturer's firmware configuration, not a physical measurement of this unit. The downloaded binary and disassembly were temporary inspection artifacts and are not committed.

The sampler uses sixteen calibrated ADC readings every fifteen seconds. Three fresh readings <=3.60 V show “Battery low / Please charge”; <=3.40 V escalates to “Battery very low / Please charge now”. Tap the banner to dismiss for fifteen minutes; critical escalation bypasses dismissal. Three readings >=3.75 V clear it. The shared high-contrast banner wakes the display without changing playback, volume, library, progress or sync. Failed calibration/ADC, saturation and implausible readings mean unknown and do not fabricate zero percent. Startup remains offline and no network/task is added.

No verified charger-status signal exists in the current adapter. USB serial host presence is not treated as charging; the donor's >4.15 V heuristic is not reused. Voltage recovery clears the warning while charging raises voltage. No charge percentage or remaining runtime is claimed.

Validation: 38 shared ASan/UBSan tests passed, including production policy/ADC failure handling and actual LVGL rendering/click handling at all three device sizes. PearlPod `tools/check.sh` and ESP-IDF 5.5.5 `tools/build.sh` passed. The existing codec, volume, startup, library, sync and power paths were preserved. App/merged images are generated in ignored dist; ordinary updates should use app-only flashing to preserve preferences. No flash or audible playback was performed for this task.

Physical acceptance remains: compare voltage to the actual cell, observe a real low-battery warning/dismissal/escalation during listening, inspect physical text/controls and charge until recovery while preserving playback. Factory firmware evidence does not establish pack calibration, charging detection, capacity or remaining listening hours.
