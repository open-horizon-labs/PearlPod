# Listener Player

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Embedded native ESP32-S3, 460×460 AMOLED touchscreen and two physical buttons.

## Users
Listener, listener, independently chooses and enjoys her music. Her parent prepares the microSD music collection and maintains firmware.

## Product Purpose
An offline music player that Listener wants to use and can operate without repeated coaching.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Multiple albums and nested directories, album art, touchscreen navigation and playback selection, 3.5 mm audio, two-button volume, long-hold power, fast startup. No Wi-Fi sync in this version. Preserve and verify factory recovery before flashing.

## Brand Commitments
Personal anime imagery inspired by Sample soundtrack and Sample collection. Readable controls and album artwork take priority over decorative animation.

## Evidence
User's explicit brief and preferences; upstream display/touch example and board pin maps; serial identification confirms ESP32-S3 revision 0.2, 8 MB PSRAM and 16 MB flash. DAC variant and power circuit remain to be verified.
